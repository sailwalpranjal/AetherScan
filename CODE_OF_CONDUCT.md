# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.

## What I expect

- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work.
- Respect that people have different experience levels and different reasons for being here.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sexualised language or attention nobody asked for.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person unwelcome in the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and can block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email pranjalsailwal09@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.
